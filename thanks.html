Thank you — your licence is on its way.

The key is in your email. Here is what to do with it.

  1. 01
    Find the key in your inbox

    It arrives from Polar within a minute and looks like TLPTH-…. If it is not there, check spam.

  2. 02
    Open Telepath

    Not installed yet? Download it, drag it to Applications and sign in to Telegram.

  3. 03
    Paste the key and press Activate

    The field is on the main screen, under the cloud. That is all — storing files is unlocked on this Mac.

One key works on two Macs. Changing computers? Release the old one in your Polar customer portal — the link is in the same email — and the key is free to use again.
